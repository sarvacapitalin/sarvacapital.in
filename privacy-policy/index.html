Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Applies to sarvacapital.in and its subdomains

1. Overview

Sarva Capital (“we”, “our”, or “us”) operates sarvacapital.in along with subdomains including llr.sarvacapital.in and sarvatv.sarvacapital.in. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit our websites.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require user registration or collect personal account profiles on our public web tools. Application preferences and study progress (such as language selection or practice history on llr.sarvacapital.in) are stored locally in your own browser via localStorage.

Standard web hosting infrastructure may automatically record basic technical request data (such as IP address, browser type, referring URL, and timestamps) for security, abuse prevention, and reliability.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.
  • For more details on how Google manages data in its ad products, please review Google’s Advertising & Privacy documentation.

4. External Links

Our pages may contain links to third-party or official government websites for reference. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

5. Contact Information
