About Sarva Capital

Independent digital product studio focused on accessible civic utilities, educational tools, and web applications.

Who We Are

Sarva Capital develops and maintains free-to-use web applications designed to solve everyday problems with clarity and speed. Rather than gating useful resources behind sign-up walls, we focus on lightweight static web experiences that work reliably across mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.

What We Build

  • Telangana RTA LLR Practice (llr.sarvacapital.in): A comprehensive study and mock-test portal helping Learner’s Licence applicants prepare in English, Telugu, and Hindi with road-sign references, traffic-rule explanations, and realistic exam simulations.
  • Sarva TV (sarvatv.sarvacapital.in): A clean, browser-based live media and streaming directory built for effortless viewing.

Editorial & Quality Standards

We review educational content regularly against official public transport and regulatory publications, and we design our interfaces to prioritize readability, accessibility, and non-intrusive user experiences.
